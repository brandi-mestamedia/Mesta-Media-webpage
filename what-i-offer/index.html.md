## Experience that moves business.

### Business development support

When you need someone to give new business consistent attention.

You have an established offering, but finding prospective customers, preparing proposals, and following up compete with everything else on your desk.

I work alongside you to focus the effort and help move qualified opportunities toward a decision.

How I help

- Define the types of customers that fit your offering.
- Research prospective accounts and relevant decision-makers.
- Develop outreach around a specific reason to connect.
- Lead or support discovery conversations.
- Prepare tailored recommendations and proposals.
- Coordinate follow-up and support negotiation.

What you receive

An agreed account focus, outreach approach, opportunity tracking, and hands-on support with the sales activities included in our scope.

### Partnership & account growth

When you want to build more business from relationships that already have a foundation.

Your existing clients may have new priorities. A potential partner may offer a way to reach a relevant audience or deliver a more useful service.

I help you explore those opportunities and develop a clear recommendation for both sides.

How I help

- Review selected client accounts and upcoming renewals.
- Prepare and support conversations about changing needs.
- Identify relevant opportunities to expand an account.
- Develop renewal and partnership proposals.
- Research potential partners and assess mutual fit.
- Clarify the contribution, expectations, and next steps for each business.

What you receive

Account development plans, renewal recommendations, or a defined partnership proposal, depending on the engagement.

### Positioning & sales materials

When customers need a clearer explanation of what you do and why it matters.

Your team knows the offering well. Your presentation or proposal may not make its value as clear to someone seeing it for the first time.

I help organize the message around the customer’s problem, your recommended solution, and the reasons to choose your business.

How I help

- Review your offering, audience, and existing materials.
- Clarify the problems your service addresses.
- Develop B2B positioning and customer-facing messaging.
- Create sales presentations and media kits.
- Build reusable proposal templates.
- Develop interactive HTML proposals where the format supports the sales conversation.

What you receive

The agreed presentation, messaging, or proposal resources, ready for your team to use and maintain.

### Practical AI & digital support

When you want a more consistent way to handle sales preparation and materials.

AI can help with research and drafting. Making it useful requires clear inputs, a defined task, and someone who knows how to evaluate the result.

I help apply these tools to specific parts of your sales process.

How I help

- Develop prompts for defined research and communication tasks.
- Build repeatable meeting-preparation and proposal workflows.
- Organize business information for more consistent drafts.
- Create sales resources and interactive proposals.
- Document how to use the resource and review its output.

What you receive

A defined workflow or digital resource, supporting instructions, and a walkthrough for the people using it.
